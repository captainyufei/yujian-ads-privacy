Yujian Ads API

Privacy Policy

Yujian Future (Shenzhen) Technology Co., Ltd

Effective date and last updated: November 5, 2026

Yujian Ads API is an internal tool for analyzing our company's own Amazon advertising performance and managing campaign bids and budgets.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Yujian Future (Shenzhen) Technology Co., Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") operates Yujian Ads API. This policy explains the intended collection, use, protection, retention, and deletion of information in connection with this internal tool and this privacy policy page.

The tool is intended solely for advertising accounts belonging to our company. Amazon Ads API functionality is used only after the required API approval and advertiser authorization have been obtained.

2. Information the tool may access

Within the permissions granted through Amazon's authorization process, the tool may access:

  • Advertising account and profile identifiers, marketplace information, account names, currencies, and time zones.
  • Campaigns, ad groups, advertised products, keywords, search terms, targeting settings, campaign status, bids, and budgets.
  • Advertising performance reports, including impressions, clicks, spend, attributed purchases, attributed sales, and related performance metrics.
  • OAuth credentials and authorization tokens required to connect to the authorized advertising accounts.
  • Operational records needed to diagnose API errors and review campaign-management actions.

The tool does not request Amazon account passwords. Its advertising use case does not require buyer names, buyer email addresses, telephone numbers, or shipping addresses.

3. How we use information

We use authorized advertising information for our own internal business operations, including downloading and consolidating reports, analyzing advertising performance, reviewing keywords and targeting, and making or automating changes to campaign bids and budgets within the authorized accounts.

We also use information to maintain the integration, investigate errors, and review advertising-management actions. We limit access and use to the information needed for these purposes.

4. Access and disclosure

Access is limited to authorized personnel who need the information for the purposes described above. Service providers may process information only as necessary to host, maintain, or secure the tool and subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

We do not sell advertising data, make it publicly available, or use it to provide advertising services to unrelated third parties. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the security of our systems and authorized accounts.

5. Security

Our security requirements for this integration include HTTPS for communications with Amazon APIs, access restrictions for authorized personnel, and protection of stored authorization credentials. Credentials must be kept outside public repositories and this public website, and operational logs must avoid exposing authorization secrets.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and deletion

Advertising information is retained only for as long as necessary for the internal analysis and management purposes described in this policy, or to satisfy applicable legal and recordkeeping obligations. Information that is no longer needed is deleted or de-identified.

Authorization credentials are retained only while needed for the authorized integration. If authorization is revoked, the tool must stop accessing the account, and credentials that are no longer needed must be removed. Authorized company representatives may contact us to request review or deletion of retained information, subject to applicable legal obligations.

7. Authorization and account control

Account owners and administrators control access through Amazon's authorization and account-permission settings and may revoke the tool's access through Amazon. Questions about access, corrections, retention, or deletion can be directed to the contact below.

8. This privacy policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not include our own analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or forms on this page. GitHub may collect technical visitor information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. For details, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the tool or our data-handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.
